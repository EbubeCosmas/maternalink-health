Why MATERNALINK HEALTH?

For many women, maternal healthcare can be fragmented. We bridge the gaps so mothers remain connected to appropriate care.

Continuity of Care

Maternal healthcare should not end when a woman leaves a facility. We support continuous connection throughout pregnancy and postpartum recovery.

Timely Follow-Up

Helping mothers keep track of scheduled appointments and important follow-up activities, extending attention beyond childbirth.

Care Coordination

Working towards better connections between mothers, healthcare providers, and appropriate maternal healthcare referral services.

About MATERNALINK HEALTH TECHNOLOGIES

MATERNALINK HEALTH is a Nigerian maternal health technology company developing a connected digital platform to support continuity of maternal care during pregnancy and after childbirth.
