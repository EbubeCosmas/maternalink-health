About Us

Who we are and why MATERNALINK HEALTH exists.

About MATERNALINK HEALTH TECHNOLOGIES

MATERNALINK HEALTH is a Nigerian maternal health technology company developing a connected digital platform to support continuity of maternal care during pregnancy and after childbirth.

We recognise that maternal healthcare should not end when a woman leaves a healthcare facility after an appointment. Mothers need reliable information, appropriate follow up, access to healthcare services and a clear connection to support when concerns arise.

We are building a platform designed to strengthen the connection between mothers, healthcare providers and maternal healthcare facilities.

Our work is focused on improving maternal care coordination, supporting timely follow up and helping address gaps in the maternal healthcare journey, particularly in underserved communities.

We believe every mother deserves to remain connected to the care she needs throughout pregnancy and postpartum recovery.

Our Mission

To strengthen continuity of maternal care by connecting pregnant and postpartum women with reliable information, appropriate healthcare services, timely follow up and referral support.

Our Vision

A future where every mother can remain connected to the care and support she needs throughout pregnancy and postpartum recovery, regardless of where she lives.

The Problem We Are Addressing

Why MATERNALINK HEALTH TECHNOLOGIES?

For many women, maternal healthcare can be fragmented. Follow up after antenatal visits may be inconsistent, important health information may be difficult to access, and referral pathways may not always be clear.

These gaps can make it difficult for mothers to remain connected to appropriate care, particularly in underserved communities.

MATERNALINK HEALTH is being developed to help address these challenges by supporting better maternal health information, follow up and coordination throughout pregnancy and after childbirth.

We believe that continuity of care is an essential part of protecting maternal wellbeing.

Who We Serve

MATERNALINK HEALTH is being developed to support:

  • Pregnant Women

    Women who need accessible maternal health information, organised care support and better connections to healthcare services.

  • Postpartum Mothers

    Women who need continued attention, follow-up and appropriate support after childbirth.

  • Maternal Healthcare Providers and Facilities

    Healthcare professionals and facilities that may benefit from improved maternal care documentation, follow up and coordination.

  • Public Health Agencies and Development Organisations

    Institutions and organisations working to strengthen maternal healthcare delivery and improve maternal health outcomes.

Our Approach

Building Better Connections in Maternal Healthcare

We believe that improving maternal healthcare requires more than providing information. It requires strengthening the connections between mothers, healthcare providers and the services available to them.

Our approach is centred on:

  • Supporting continuity of care throughout pregnancy and postpartum recovery.
  • Encouraging timely maternal health follow up.
  • Improving access to reliable maternal health information.
  • Supporting referral and coordination pathways.
  • Developing solutions that respond to the realities of underserved communities.
We are starting with a proposed pilot in Delta State, Nigeria, with the long-term goal of expanding our reach to other states in Nigeria and Africa as the platform develops and is validated.

About the Founder

Founded with a Commitment to Maternal Wellbeing

MATERNALINK HEALTH TECHNOLOGIES was founded by a Nigerian licensed pharmacist with a Doctor of Pharmacy degree from the University of Benin and approximately seven years of experience in both hospital and community pharmacy practice.

Her professional experience has strengthened her interest in accessible healthcare, patient support and continuity of care.

The idea behind MATERNALINK HEALTH is driven by the recognition that mothers need more than occasional healthcare visits. They need reliable information, appropriate follow up and connections to care throughout pregnancy and after childbirth.

Through MATERNALINK HEALTH, she is working to develop a practical, technology enabled approach to strengthening maternal healthcare coordination, beginning in Nigeria.
