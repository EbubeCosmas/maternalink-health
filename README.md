# Maternalink Health

A digital maternal screening, referral, and follow-up platform designed to bridge the gap between pregnant mothers, community health workers, and healthcare facilities.

## 🚀 Live Demo
Check out the live application here: [Maternalink Health Platform](https://maternalinkhealth.com)

---

## 📌 Features
* **Patient Registration & Screening:** Seamless intake for expectant and postpartum mothers, supporting both self-registration and assisted entry via community health workers.
* **Clinical Decision Support:** Automated calculation of gestational age, trimesters, and expected delivery dates (EDD).
* **Referral Tracking:** Generates de-identified referral tokens to connect patients safely with participating healthcare facilities.
* **Multilingual Follow-Up:** WhatsApp and SMS reminder preferences supporting English, Nigerian Pidgin, Igbo, Yoruba, and Hausa.

---
